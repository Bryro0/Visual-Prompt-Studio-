Album Cover

Heavy Metal Gothic

Create a promotional album cover for a band. The Genre is Heavy Metal with gothic aesthetics. In the foreground of the composition to depict a central figure. Make it so that the crow is on the right side of the image, leaving ample room to see the background. There should be a crow with its beak slightly agape. On the floor in front of it should be a worn-out cigarette. The crow should show interest in the cigarette as if it is going to pick it up with its beak. The environment should show that the crow is outside in a newly built cemetery, black brick underneath the crow with a sheen to it. The cemetery should be overall simple while still showing the gothic aesthetics. Tombstones should be scattered about. Show that it is evening out with deep subtle blues. 
In the background on the left side should show an old skeleton sitting up but, in a pose, suggesting a sudden death. The skeleton should wear gaudy seemingly expensive gothic style clothing, though the clothing should be shown to be aged a bit. This skeleton should be in a small mausoleum. The opening of the mausoleum should be the darkest area in the composition with shadows. Only the skeleton should be visible while still blending into the dark. 
Beside the skeleton, show a silver cigarette case. This case should contrast the lack of light in the mausoleum by catching light from the outside, causing it to glimmer and draw the viewers attention to it. Show a line of light leading up to it.

Glossary

Value - The light and dark areas of a compositions coloring. Can create shadow and lighting effects.

Space - The areas in an art piece that surround it discernable elements. Can be both negative and positive.

Unity - How the various elements of an artwork work together harmoniously 

Balance - How Visual weight is distributed across an artwork. Can show asymmetry and symmetry for variety. 

Rhythm - Elements of art that help guide the viewers eyes across the composition.
